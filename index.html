Hold up, what's Pnchy?

What is Pnchy?

Pnchy is the app that turns your regulars into your best marketing. One digital punch card for every local spot you love: barbershops, cafes, bars, comic shops, record stores, and more. Real rewards for actually showing up. No app-hopping between five different loyalty apps, no plastic card you lose in a drawer.

What is a digital punch card app?

Same idea as the paper card taped to the register, minus the paper. Scan a QR code at checkout, get stamped, watch your reward bar fill up. Merchants can optionally use that same QR to process the transaction too, no need to rip out an existing POS system if you'd rather keep it. It's instant, it can't be faked or photocopied, and it's not getting lost in the bottom of your bag.

How does this help small businesses?

Most independent shops have zero idea who their regulars are, or when a regular quietly turns into a one-time visit. That's expensive: Harvard Business Review found that boosting customer retention by just 5% can lift profits 25 to 95%, since keeping a regular costs far less than chasing a new customer. It's bigger than any one shop, too. Small businesses generate 44% of all U.S. economic activity, according to the Small Business Administration. They're the actual pillar of a neighborhood, not just a stat. Pnchy gives owners real visit data, automatic pings to win back lapsed customers, and a loyalty program that actually feels rewarding. No hardware to buy, no POS system to switch, free to start.

How is Pnchy different from points-based loyalty systems?

Points systems make you do math to feel rewarded, and most people quit before it pays off. McKinsey research found that 77% of points-based loyalty programs fail within two years because they're just not compelling enough to keep people engaged. Pnchy is visit-based and social instead. You're not collecting points in a vacuum, you're climbing a neighborhood leaderboard and unlocking group rewards with other regulars. It's giving community, not spreadsheet.

What is a Density Drop?

A Density Drop is a reward that unlocks when enough people individually put in the time. A merchant sets the headcount goal, Pnchy sets the time window (usually around 3 hours), and each person has to dwell in the spot for a set number of minutes to count. Once enough people have clocked their dwell time within that window, everyone who showed up gets rewarded. Flash-mob energy, but it's just your favorite comic book store on a Wednesday.

Why are we ditching reviews for discovery and using foot-traffic leaderboards paired with friend-only reviews?

Star ratings are broken. Anyone can leave five stars without ever walking through the door, and it's such a widespread problem that the FTC finalized a federal rule in 2024 banning fake reviews and testimonials outright. Pnchy ranks spots by verified foot traffic instead: real people, physically showing up, over and over. And the only opinions you actually see are from friends you trust, not strangers with an agenda, which lines up with Nielsen's research that 92% of people trust recommendations from friends and family over any form of advertising. Discovery should feel like a recommendation from your group chat, not a coin flip.

How do I get my favorite local shop onto Pnchy?

Search for them in the widget above or on our petition page and sign. That's your nomination. Once enough people ask for the same business, we reach out directly to bring them onboard. The petition is literally how we decide who gets onto Pnchy first.
