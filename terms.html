6. Pnches and rewards

A Pnch is a loyalty credit at one business. It has no cash value, cannot be sold or transferred, and does not carry over between businesses. A shopper earns at most one Pnch per business per day for purchases. If a payment is refunded or disputed, the Pnch it earned is reversed.

Each business decides its own reward and the number of Pnches it takes, and it can change or end its program. To claim a reward, you request it in your shopper account and the business confirms it.
