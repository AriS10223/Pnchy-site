Privacy Policy

Pnchy, LLC ("Pnchy", "we", "us", or "our") provides a community loyalty and payments service for local businesses and the people who shop with them. This Privacy Policy describes how we process personal information that we collect through the Pnchy website, the Pnchy pay pages and shopper account, and the Pnchy app for businesses (together, the "Service"), and through our related communications.

California Notice at Collection / State Privacy Rights Notice: see section 17 below for important information about your rights under applicable state privacy laws.

CCPA / CPRA US State Laws United States only App Store Google Play

The short version

We never sell your data and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Stripe handles your card. Pnchy and the business never see or store your full card number, security code, or PIN.

Businesses see "Customer #12", not your email address. A receipt is not a marketing sign-up.

You can delete your account inside the app at any time, or by email (see section 9).

Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google. Pnchy never sees your card number or your Apple or Google account details, only the status of a plan and an anonymous purchase ID tied to your business.

We do not track you across other companies' apps and websites. Our apps do not ask for permission to track you because we do not do it.

5. What businesses can see

A business can see the payments it took, the amounts, tips, discounts, and refunds, and its customers' loyalty activity. It sees each customer only as an anonymous label such as "Customer #12", including on disputes and refund requests about its payments. We do not give a business your email address, and a business cannot look up your history at other businesses. A business may have its own information from dealing with you in person, and it is responsible for how it uses that.

Providing your email for a receipt, paying, or earning Pnches does not enroll you in marketing. If you do opt in to a business's emails, the email is sent by Pnchy on the business's behalf, and the business does not receive your address.

If you ask for a refund, the business also sees your request: the reason, your description, and your photos, under the same anonymous label, plus the amount and date of the payment. Your description or photos may contain anything you choose to include, so keep personal details out of them.

Content you submit. Content that business owners submit, such as a business name, reward text, and email campaign text, is stored by us, shown to customers on pay pages, receipts, and emails, and may be checked automatically or reviewed by us to enforce our Terms of Use, including the rules against objectionable content. If you report content, we use the report and the information in it to investigate and respond. We may keep removed content and related records for as long as needed to enforce our terms, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

6. Pnchy Pro: subscriptions, branding, and sending domains

Pnchy Pro is an optional paid plan for business owners. Here is how it affects your information.

Subscriptions. Pnchy Pro is sold only through the Apple App Store and Google Play. The store charges you and keeps your payment details. We receive signed purchase and renewal messages from the store so we can start, keep, or end Pro, and we link a plan to one business with an anonymous identifier, not your name or email. We use these records to provide Pro, prevent misuse, handle refunds and disputes with the stores, and meet tax and accounting obligations.

Branding. The logo, banner text, and colors you add are used in emails to your customers, and we may check or review them to enforce our Terms of Use. A logo is stored where anyone with its link can open it, because email programs must be able to load it, so do not upload anything private. We remove image metadata when we re-encode a logo.

Your own sending domain. If you send from your own domain, we register it with our email delivery provider and keep its name, your chosen sender name, and the DNS records and status that provider gives us. Email goes out from your domain only after every record is verified. A domain that is not verified within 72 hours is released. If your plan ends, email returns to Pnchy's address straight away, and we remove the domain 30 days later.

Pnchy news. If you join the Pnchy list and confirm it, we may send you Pnchy news and rewards. We record which business you joined through and use that business's plan to decide the sender: Pnchy's own address, or the business's name "via Pnchy" if that business is not on Pro. They are never sent from a business's own domain. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and businesses do not receive your email address.

When a Pro plan ends, we keep your saved branding so it comes back if you resubscribe, except that a sending domain is removed after 30 days. If a business deletes its account, its branding, logo, and sending domain are deleted too, and we keep only a subscription record, as described in section 8.

7. Disputes, amounts owed, and refund requests

Disputes and amounts owed (business owners). When a customer disputes a payment, we keep the dispute record and the evidence you submit. Evidence sent to Stripe contains purchase facts such as the date, amount, receipt delivery, and refund history, but not the customer's email address, name, billing address, or IP address. If a dispute is lost, we record the amount you owe, send notices, and may debit your Stripe balance under the debit consent you gave in the app. These records are described in section 2 and kept as described in section 8.

Refund requests (shoppers). Your receipt has a "Request a refund" link. Here is what that means for your information. The rules for the feature are in our Terms of Use.

Your receipt link is private. It holds a code that identifies your payment. Anyone with the link can see the purchase, the status and the business's reply, and can act on the request, so don't forward your receipt. You don't sign in.

Who sees what you send. The business sees your reason, words and photos as "Customer #12", never your email or name. If a card or bank request goes to Pnchy, a Pnchy staff member sees the shop name, your words and photos, the shop's reply, and the amount and date. They never see your email or your card or bank details. Staff sign in with their own accounts and open one case at a time. A few Pnchy team members with system access may also see this data to keep the Service running. Please keep personal details, other people, ID and payment cards out of your words and photos.

Photos. Your browser or phone shrinks each photo, and we remove its metadata, including any location, on our servers. Photos are stored privately with our hosting and database provider and shown to the business and to Pnchy staff only through short-lived links. They are never public, and never used for advertising or to train a model.

Emails about your request. We email the address on your receipt when we receive your request, when the business answers, when it goes to Pnchy, and when it is decided. They are not marketing. The business never gets your address. Anyone can type any address at checkout, so if you get one of these emails and didn't make the purchase, ignore it or write to hello@pnchy.app.

Why we use it. To handle your request and the refund, keep our payment records right, prevent abuse and fraud, and meet legal and accounting duties. A business or a Pnchy staff member decides every request. Nothing is decided automatically.

8. Retention

We retain personal information for as long as needed to fulfill the purposes for which we collected it, including to satisfy legal, accounting, or reporting requirements, to establish or defend legal claims, and to prevent fraud. To decide how long, we consider the amount, nature, and sensitivity of the information, the risk of harm from unauthorized use or disclosure, the purposes for which we process it and whether we can achieve them another way, and applicable legal requirements. When we no longer need it, we delete it, anonymize it, or isolate it from further processing.

Payment, receipt, and loyalty records are kept as long as needed for accounting, tax, refunds, disputes, and payment-network rules, and may be kept in anonymised form after that. Marketing preferences are kept so we can honor unsubscribes. Early access registrations and form submissions are kept for up to two years. Analytics data on the website follows Google's retention settings, which default to 14 months.

Pnchy Pro subscription records (the plan, its dates, and the store's transaction identifier) are kept for 7 years after the plan ends, including after a business deletes its account. When an account is deleted, the business's name, contact details, branding, and logo are removed, but the store's transaction identifier stays so that refunds and disputes can be matched with Apple or Google. Branding and logo files are deleted when a business deletes its account. A sending domain is removed from our records and from our email delivery provider 30 days after a plan ends, or sooner when the account is deleted. Dispute records are kept for 7 years after the dispute is closed. Amount-owed records are kept for 7 years after the business's balance last reached zero. A business's account-debit consent is kept for 7 years after a newer consent replaces it, and the latest consent is kept for as long as it is the current one. All of these are kept after a business deletes its account.

Refund request records (the reason, description, reply, status, dates, and any decision) are kept for 7 years after the request is closed, including after a business deletes its account. Photos in a refund request or reply are deleted 90 days after the request is closed. If you ask us to delete your information, we may keep a refund request record in anonymised form where we need it for accounting, to resolve a dispute, or to meet a legal duty.

9. Deleting your account

In the app. Business owners: open Settings in the Pnchy app for businesses and choose to delete your account. Shoppers: open your account page (the "me" page, reached from your emailed sign-in link) and choose to delete your account.

Without the app. You can also ask us to delete your account and data by emailing hello@pnchy.app with the subject "Delete my account" from the email address on the account. We may ask you to confirm it is you, and we respond within 30 days.

What happens. A business is deactivated and anonymised, and a shopper's personal details are anonymised. Some transaction records, and a one-way record that a device or card has paid at a business, remain so that the ledger stays accurate, refunds and disputes can be handled, and the welcome discount cannot be reset by deleting an account. Other records are kept for the periods in section 8. A business cannot delete its account while it owes an amount from disputes or refunds. Deleting your Pnchy account does not cancel an Apple or Google subscription: cancel it in the store first.
