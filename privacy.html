Privacy Policy

Pnchy is built for your neighborhood, so we believe you have a right to know exactly how your information is handled. This policy covers the Pnchy website, Pnchy Lite (the pay pages, the shopper account, and the Pnchy Lite app for businesses), and is written to be read, not buried.

GDPR UK GDPR CCPA LGPD US State Laws PIPEDA POPIA

The short version

We never sell your data and we do not share it for advertising.

Stripe handles your card. Pnchy and the business never see or store your full card number, security code, or PIN.

Businesses see "Customer #12", not your email address. A receipt is not a marketing sign-up.

You can delete your account inside the app at any time.

Who we are

Pnchy is a community loyalty and payments platform that connects local businesses with the neighborhoods they serve. Pnchy Lite is operated by Pnchy, LLC ("Pnchy", "we", "us"), mailing address 485 Foley Street, Unit 621, Somerville, MA 02145, USA, registered office 131 Continental Dr, Suite 305, Newark, Delaware 19713. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or would like to exercise your rights, contact us at hello@pnchy.app. We will respond within 30 days.

For payments and loyalty, Pnchy decides why and how your information is used, and we are responsible for it under this policy. The business you pay is separately responsible for how it uses what you tell its staff.

We do not collect government-issued identification or sensitive personal data as defined under applicable law, and we do not ask for your full card number, security code, or PIN. Do not include sensitive information in any content you submit.

How we use your information

Providing the service: Processing payments, sending receipts, calculating discounts, tracking Pnches and rewards, and supporting refunds and disputes.

Security and fraud prevention: Detecting stolen cards, repeated or abusive requests, and misuse of charge codes, and enforcing limits.

Marketing, only with your confirmation: Emails from a business or from Pnchy are sent only after a separate confirmation (double opt-in). You can unsubscribe at any time, without penalty.

Improving Pnchy: Understanding how the product and website are used so we can fix problems and build better features. Website analytics run only after you consent.

Legal compliance: Satisfying tax, accounting, payment-network, and other legal obligations, and responding to lawful requests.

What businesses can see

A business can see the payments it took, the amounts, tips, discounts, and refunds, and its customers' loyalty activity. It sees each customer only as an anonymous label such as "Customer #12". We do not give a business your email address, and a business cannot look up your history at other businesses. A business may have its own information from dealing with you in person, and it is responsible for how it uses that.

Providing your email for a receipt, paying, or earning Pnches does not enroll you in marketing. If you do opt in to a business's emails, the email is sent by Pnchy on the business's behalf, and the business does not receive your address.

Content you submit

Content that business owners submit, such as a business name, reward text, and email campaign text, is stored by us, shown to customers on pay pages, receipts, and emails, and may be checked automatically or reviewed by us to enforce our Terms of Use, including the rules against objectionable content. If you report content, we use the report and the information in it to investigate and respond. We may keep removed content and related records for as long as needed to enforce our terms, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

Google Analytics (website only)

We use Google Analytics 4 on the Pnchy marketing website to understand which pages are visited, how long visitors spend on them, and their approximate geographic region. It collects only anonymized and aggregated data and is not used to collect names, email addresses, or payment information. Pnchy Lite pay pages and apps do not use Google Analytics.

Analytics are activated only after you provide explicit consent through our cookie banner. If you decline, Google Analytics operates in cookieless mode and no tracking cookies are placed on your device. You may withdraw consent at any time by clearing the pnchy_cookie_consent value from your browser's local storage.

How we share your information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context advertising. We share it only with the following:

Stripe: Processes card, wallet, and bank payments and verifies businesses, under its own privacy policy (stripe.com/privacy).

Supabase: Hosts our database, authentication, and server functions.

Resend: Delivers receipts, confirmations, and campaign emails.

PostHog: Receives product usage events. Business owners are identified only by an internal account ID, and shoppers are never identified.

Expo, Apple, and Google: Deliver push notifications to the business app and distribute the app and in-app subscriptions. Apple Pay and Google Pay involve the wallet provider you choose.

Hosting and analytics providers: Our websites are hosted by Vercel (Pnchy Lite) and GitHub Pages (this site), and, if you consent, Google processes anonymized website analytics.

Legal or regulatory bodies and business transfers: We may disclose information where required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Pnchy or our users, and as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice to you.

These providers act on our instructions and may use your information only to provide their services to us, except Stripe, which also uses payment information as an independent controller as described in its policy.

International transfers

Pnchy is based in the United States and our providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for transfers, such as standard contractual clauses.

Regulations we comply with

Pnchy is designed to respect applicable privacy laws across multiple jurisdictions. Below is a summary of how each framework applies to us.

GDPR

European Union. All data subject rights apply. We rely on consent for analytics and marketing, and on contract and legitimate interests for payments and fraud prevention.

UK GDPR

United Kingdom. The same framework as the EU GDPR applies following the UK's exit from the European Union.

CCPA / CPRA

California. We do not sell or share your personal data. You have the right to know, correct, delete, and limit.

LGPD

Brazil. Processing is based on consent, contract, and legitimate interest. You have the right to access, correct, and delete your data.

US State Laws

Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, Oregon, and others. Opt-out and access rights are honored for all residents.

PIPEDA

Canada. We collect data only with your knowledge and consent. You may access and correct your data upon request.

POPIA

South Africa. We adhere to the principles of lawful processing and data minimization as required by the Act.

Under GDPR and UK GDPR our legal bases are: consent (Article 6(1)(a)) for analytics and marketing; performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b)) for payments, receipts, accounts, and loyalty; legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) for security and fraud prevention; and legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)) for tax and accounting records.

Data retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes in this policy. Payment, receipt, and loyalty records are kept as long as needed for accounting, tax, refunds, disputes, and payment-network rules, and may be kept in anonymised form after that. Marketing preferences are kept so we can honor unsubscribes. Early access registrations and form submissions are kept for up to two years. Analytics data on the website follows Google's retention settings, which default to 14 months.

When you delete your account, a business is deactivated and anonymised, and a shopper's personal details are anonymised. Some transaction records and a one-way record that a device or card has paid at a business remain so that the ledger stays accurate, refunds and disputes can be handled, and the welcome discount cannot be reset by deleting an account.

Children

Pnchy is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact hello@pnchy.app and we will delete it.

Security

We use industry-standard technical and organizational measures: encryption in transit, access controls that restrict who can read data, rate limits against abuse, and payment handling by Stripe, which is PCI DSS Level 1 certified. No system is entirely immune to risk. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

Privacy disputes

Any remaining dispute about this policy is governed by the dispute and arbitration terms in our Terms of Use, including individual arbitration, the class action waiver, and the right to opt out within 30 days, except where the law gives you rights that cannot be waived, such as the right to complain to a data protection authority.
