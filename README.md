## What Is Pnchy?

Pnchy is a QR payment and loyalty platform for farmers market vendors, pop-ups and small shops. A customer scans the vendor's QR code, enters the short code the vendor created for the sale and pays on a web page with no app to download. Every purchase earns Pnches toward a reward the vendor sets.

- **QR payments:** card, Apple Pay, Google Pay and bank transfer (ACH), or cash confirmed in the app.
- **Loyalty:** Pnches, a vendor-set reward and an optional welcome discount for first-time customers.
- **Retention:** double opt-in email campaigns. Vendors never see customer email addresses.
- **Tap to Pay and Pro:** Tap to Pay for in-person cards without a reader; optional Pro ($3.99/mo or $35.99/yr) for branded receipts, own sending domain and higher email campaign limits.
- **Business app (iOS and Android):** live payments, customers, campaigns, analytics and push alerts.

## Pricing

Founding vendors pay no Pnchy fee. Card and bank payments still carry Stripe's processing cost; Pnchy deducts an estimate of it with no markup. Cash carries no processing cost.

## How Pnchy Differs

Square, Shopify POS, Toast and Clover are full point-of-sale systems. Pnchy is a lighter layer for a stall: QR payment on a web page with loyalty included and no card reader needed. See `llms-full.txt` for a dated, sourced comparison.
